### Research Transparency

Published estimates are intended for workforce planning.

They are not official government statistics, guaranteed
salary rates, verified payroll survey results, or guaranteed
visa-processing timelines.

Sources, assumptions, methodology and update dates should
accompany datasets published in this repository.
